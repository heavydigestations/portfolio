Heavy Digestations are Liverpool-based artists and writers–their interdisciplinary practice is "culino-philisophico-autobiographical," a quote borrowed from The Debt to Pleasure by John Lancaster. Using low-resolution and low-culture mediums and aesthetics, they build speculative post-humanist worlds that examine the erotics of consumption, digital landscapes, and presentations of the self.
